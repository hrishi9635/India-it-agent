Sector view

Coforge led the pack with 53.6% YoY revenue growth and raised its EBIT margin guidance. LTM and Tech Mahindra also delivered double‑digit growth. Infosys and Wipro showed the weakest top‑line expansion, and Wipro's profit slipped. AI‑related revenue and deal wins were highlighted across most companies, but specific figures were limited.

Leading

  • Coforge – 53.6% YoY revenue growth and raised EBIT margin guidance to 16%
  • LTM – 13.6% YoY revenue growth and strong AI‑led managed services deals
  • Tech Mahindra – 15.6% YoY revenue growth with solid profit expansion

Under pressure

  • Infosys – Only 1.5% YoY revenue growth and profit growth slowed to 6%
  • Wipro – Revenue growth slowed to 9.1% YoY and net profit fell 1.9%
  • OFSS – Modest 7% YoY revenue growth and low scale
