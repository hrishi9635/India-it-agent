Sector view

Revenue rose across the board, with TCS, HCLTech and Tech Mahindra posting double‑digit YoY gains. Profit growth was uneven; TCS and Coforge delivered strong margins, while Infosys trimmed guidance and Persistent saw profit decline. AI revenue is becoming a material contributor, especially for HCLTech and Wipro. Dividend payouts remained robust for most peers.

Leading

  • TCS – Highest revenue and operating margin, strong profit growth.
  • Coforge – 53.6% YoY revenue growth and raised EBIT margin guidance.
  • HCLTech – AI‑driven sales lifted profit 21% YoY and solid margin expansion.

Under pressure

  • Persistent – Revenue grew but profit fell 27% QoQ, indicating cost pressure.
  • Infosys – Revenue growth slowed to 1.5% YoY and guidance was cut.
  • Wipro – Profit slipped despite revenue growth, margin flat.
