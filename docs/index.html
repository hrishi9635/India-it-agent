Sector view

Revenue grew across the sector, led by Coforge's 53.6% YoY surge and HCLTech's 11.5% rise. Profit growth was uneven; Infosys and HCLTech posted double‑digit profit gains, while Wipro saw a 1.9% decline. Guidance remained cautious, with Infosys trimming FY27 growth to 1‑2% and HCLTech introducing a 2.5‑4% range. AI contributions were highlighted but quantified only by a few firms.

Leading

  • Coforge – Revenue jumped 53.6% YoY and EBIT margin guidance was raised to 16%.
  • HCLTech – Revenue up 11.5% YoY, profit up 21%, and strong AI revenue contribution.

Under pressure

  • Wipro – Revenue grew 9.1% YoY but profit fell 1.9% and headcount was reduced.
  • Infosys – Revenue growth slowed to 1.5% YoY and profit growth was only 6% while guidance was cut.
