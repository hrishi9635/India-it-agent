Sector view

Revenue growth ranged from low single‑digits to over 50% YoY. Coforge led with 53.6% growth, while Infosys trimmed its FY27 guidance to 1‑2% and posted only 11% YoY growth. Profit margins improved for most firms, driven by AI‑related deals, but Wipro saw a 1.9% profit decline. Guidance remained steady for most peers, with HCLTech and Wipro introducing new FY27 outlooks.

Leading

  • Coforge – Revenue surged 53.6% YoY and EBIT margin guidance raised to 16%.
  • Persistent – Revenue grew 39.9% YoY, the fastest among listed peers.

Under pressure

  • Infosys – Revenue growth slowed to 11% YoY and FY27 guidance cut to 1‑2%.
  • Wipro – Net profit fell 1.9% YoY despite 9.1% revenue growth.
