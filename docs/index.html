Sector view

Revenue grew overall, led by Coforge's 53.6% YoY surge and solid performance from LTM and Tech Mahindra. Profit growth was uneven; HCLTech posted a 21% profit jump, but Wipro and Persistent saw declines. AI contributions were highlighted by several firms but concrete revenue figures were limited. Guidance remained modest, with Infosys trimming FY27 growth expectations and HCLTech introducing a 2.5‑4.0% FY27 target.

Leading

  • Coforge – 53.6% YoY revenue growth and raised EBIT margin guidance to 16%
  • HCLTech – 11.5% revenue growth and 21% profit increase, driven by AI
  • Tech Mahindra – 15.6% revenue growth and strong profit expansion

Under pressure

  • Wipro – Revenue grew 9.1% YoY but profit fell 1.9%
  • Persistent – Profit dropped 25% YoY despite 20% revenue growth
  • Infosys – Revenue growth slowed to 1.5% YoY and guidance trimmed

Big themes

AI‑driven revenue
