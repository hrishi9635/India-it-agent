Sector view

Revenue growth ranged from modest double‑digit for the majors to over 50% for Coforge. Profit margins improved for HCLTech and Coforge, while Infosys and Wipro saw profit pressure. Guidance was trimmed by Infosys and introduced by HCLTech and Wipro. AI revenue contributions were highlighted across most companies.

Leading

  • Coforge – Revenue surged 53.6% YoY and EBIT margin guidance was raised to 16%.
  • LTM – 13.6% revenue growth with AI revenue up 47% YoY.
  • HCLTech – Profit jumped 21% YoY, driven by strong AI revenue.

Under pressure

  • Infosys – Revenue growth slowed to 1.5% YoY and profit margin guidance was cut.
  • Wipro – Revenue grew 9.1% but profit fell 1.9% and margin pressure persisted.
  • Persistent – Revenue grew 30% but profit dropped 25% YoY, indicating cost issues.
