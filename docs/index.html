Sector view

Revenue growth ranged from sub‑2% at Infosys to over 50% at Coforge. Profit margins improved at HCLTech and Coforge, while Infosys trimmed guidance and Wipro saw profit dip. AI‑related deal wins were highlighted by several firms, but only a few disclosed concrete AI revenue figures.

Leading

  • Coforge – 53.6% YoY revenue growth and raised EBIT margin guidance to 16%.
  • HCLTech – 11.5% YoY revenue growth, 21% profit jump and AI revenue of $219 m.

Under pressure

  • Infosys – Revenue grew only 1.5% YoY and FY27 guidance was cut to 1‑2%.
  • Wipro – Revenue grew 9.1% YoY but profit fell 1.9% and headcount declined.
