Sector view

Top‑line growth was strong, led by Coforge's 66% YoY revenue jump and TCS's 15% rise. Profit margins diverged, with HCLTech improving to 18.2% while Infosys trimmed guidance to 1‑2% growth. AI revenue accelerated, contributing 10%+ of total revenue for TCS and 78% YoY growth for HCLTech. Guidance was mixed, with only Wipro providing a forward outlook.

Leading

  • Coforge – Revenue surged 66% YoY and EBIT margin guidance raised to 16%.
  • TCS – Largest revenue base with 15% YoY growth and 24% operating margin.

Under pressure

  • Infosys – Revenue growth slowed to 1.5% YoY and guidance trimmed to 1‑2%.
  • Wipro – Revenue grew only 9% YoY and net profit fell 2%.
