Sector view

Revenue rose across the board, led by Coforge's 53.6% YoY surge and TCS's 15.4% increase. Operating margins stayed strong at TCS (24%) and Coforge (16%) but slipped for Infosys and Wipro. Guidance was trimmed by Infosys and introduced by Wipro, reflecting mixed confidence. AI revenue grew sharply, especially for HCLTech and LTM.

Leading

  • Coforge – 53.6% YoY revenue growth and raised EBIT margin guidance to 16%.
  • TCS – Largest revenue base with 15.4% YoY growth and 24% operating margin.

Under pressure

  • Infosys – Only 1.5% YoY revenue growth and a cut in FY27 guidance.
  • Wipro – Revenue growth slowed to 9.1% YoY and net profit fell 1.9%.
