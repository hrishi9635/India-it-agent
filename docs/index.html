Sector view

Coforge led the quarter with over 50% revenue growth, driven by AI‑focused deals. HCLTech and TCS delivered solid top‑line growth and expanding margins, supported by AI revenue contributions. Infosys trimmed its FY27 guidance as revenue growth slowed to 1‑2%. Wipro posted revenue growth but profit fell, reflecting mixed execution.

Leading

  • Coforge – Revenue up 53.6% YoY and EBIT margin guidance raised to 16%.
  • HCLTech – Revenue grew 11.5% YoY, profit jumped 21% and AI revenue reached $219 m.
  • TCS – Steady 11.2% revenue growth, strong operating margin and AI revenue crossing 10% of total.

Under pressure

  • Infosys – Revenue growth slowed to 1.5% YoY and FY27 guidance cut to 1‑2%.
  • Wipro – Revenue grew 9.1% YoY but profit fell 1.9% and headcount reduced.
  • Infosys – Guidance cut reflects weaker top‑line momentum.
