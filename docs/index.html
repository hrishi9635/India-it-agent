Sector view

Revenue rose across the majority of the cohort, led by Coforge's 53.6% YoY surge and TCS's 15.4% increase. Profit growth was mixed; HCLTech and Coforge posted strong gains, while Infosys trimmed guidance and Persistent saw profit decline. AI revenue is becoming a material contributor, highlighted by TCS, HCLTech and Wipro. Guidance outlook is mixed, with Infosys cutting its FY27 growth target and HCLTech maintaining a modest outlook.
