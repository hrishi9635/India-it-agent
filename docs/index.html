Sector view

Revenue expansion ranged from sub‑2% at Infosys to over 50% at Coforge. AI revenue contributed materially for several firms, boosting profits at HCLTech and Mphasis. Guidance was trimmed at Infosys but raised at Coforge and Mphasis. Profit trends were mixed, with notable declines at Wipro and modest rises at most peers.
